# Seek Work wiki

Seek Work is the personal workspace included with seek-stack. It runs on the DeepSeek Harness and the local model configured by the installer. Open [Work on your PC](http://127.0.0.1:3080/work) after starting the stack. The app has conversations, background tasks, a shared browser, files, schedules, images, finance, connections, ideas, and memory.

## Start here

1. [Install and open Work](https://github.com/thejoelrobinson/seek-stack/wiki/Getting-Started).
2. [Explore the features](https://github.com/thejoelrobinson/seek-stack/wiki/Features).
3. [Give Work its first task](https://github.com/thejoelrobinson/seek-stack/wiki/Tasks-and-Conversations).
